Your mission
Something's broken.
Find it. Fix it.
The NovaPay dashboard has bugs hidden across the HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Use Copilot and your own eyes to track them down — and fix every one you can.
How to approach it
1
Open the preview. Look at the page. Does anything look obviously wrong?
2
Use the page. Try making a withdrawal. Try the interest calculator. What breaks?
3
Read the code. Open bank-dashboard.html. Read carefully — especially the JavaScript.
4
Check the console. Press F12 → Console. Any red errors?
5
Fix and test. Save your change, refresh the preview, check it worked.
