Pre-Flight Checklist
Do this the morning of — before a single student walks in
🏫The room
Arrive at least 30 minutes early
Projector on, connected, and actually showing your laptop screen
Wifi connected and confirmed fast enough to load a Codespace
Power for your laptop, plus a backup charger if you have one
💻Your own setup
Your repo URL written on the board / slide / somewhere visible all day
Open a fresh Codespace yourself, right now, and confirm it loads
Ask Copilot Chat "Who are you?" — confirm Nova responds correctly, not generically
Confirm challenge.js is in the repo with the buggy function inside
Confirm Live Server opens the dashboard preview without errors
🖨️Printed materials — physically in your hand
Master Script (or Session Day Script)
Timed Agenda — one glance, whole day
Troubleshooting Card
Bug Cheat Sheet
Student Roster — names filled in if you know them in advance
Progress Tracker — blank, ready to fill in live
Prompts Cheat Sheet
"If It All Falls Apart" page
📺On-screen files ready to open
bug-hunt-screen.html, level-up-screen.html, brainstorm-screen.html, milestone-screen.html — all open in browser tabs, in order, ready to switch between
🧘You
Water nearby
You've already run the tutorials yourself, at least once, before today
You remember: nothing here can be permanently broken, and every problem you've hit already has a written fix
If everything above is ticked, you are ready. That's not reassurance — it's the actual, literal state of things.
